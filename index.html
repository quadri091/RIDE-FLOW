FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

Everything riders and drivers usually ask before their first trip.

How do I book a ride?

Sign up as a rider, pick your pickup and drop-off on the map, then create your trip. Nearby drivers can apply for it, or you can assign it straight to a driver near you.

How do I know I'm getting into the right car?

Every trip has its own match code. Check that it matches the code your driver sees, along with the car and plate number shown on the driver's card.

Can I track my driver?

Yes. Once a driver accepts your trip you can follow them on the live map until you reach your drop-off.

Can I call or message my driver?

Yes. As soon as a driver is on your trip you can chat with them or call them right from your dashboard.

Can I cancel a trip?

Yes, you can cancel any time before the trip starts from your active trips.

Can I save places I go to often?

Yes. Save your usual pickup and drop-off and reuse them the next time you book.

What if something goes wrong on a trip?

Open a dispute on the completed trip from your dashboard. Add what happened and any pictures, and our team will review it.

How do I become a driver?

Sign up as a driver with your car details, plate number, driving licence and pictures of your car. You need to be 18 or older. Once you're in, go online to start getting trip requests.
